HeroSlider Test

The hero slider automatically cycles through slides with either parallax or fade transitions.

Features

  • Parallax mode: Previous slide zooms and collapses width
  • Fade mode: Smooth crossfade between slides
  • Z-index management: Proper layering during transitions
  • Responsive: Adapts to window resize
  • IntersectionObserver: Optimized performance

Current mode: Parallax (default)

To test fade mode, modify opts.transition.type to 'fade' in the script below
